
3D-printing of the files has been tested with the following settings:
	
	- Prusa MK2S
	- PLA
	- 0.2mm layer height
	- 0% infill
	- Support material only needed on some of the parts with large overhangs

The tolerance between the drawers and the drawer-holders has been set to 0.2mm.
This may need to be adjusted depending on the 3D-printer used.
